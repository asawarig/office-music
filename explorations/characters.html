Faces

Sixteen faces built from the same parts: hair, eyes, mouth, glasses and beards. Left is the reference's two-tone palette, right is how they'd look in the office with skin tones.

Body styles

Each shows four teammates up close, then the same four seated at desks. The shirt colour is the teammate's role colour.

Chairs

Every chair is built as real isometric blocks, so it can turn to face any of four directions. In the office view, chairs sit at different angles: tucked in, swivelled, pushed back.
