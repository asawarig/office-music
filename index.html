Office Harmony

Every teammate makes one office noise: ringing phones, papers knocked into neat stacks, the coffee machine, typing, clicking, the claims stamp, a pen on a glass, the photocopier and the washroom flush. Thirteen people, each with their own seat. Call them in and their noises lock into one groove, or switch to Ambient and let the office hum along at its own pace.

Hover over someone to see who they are. Click them to send them home, or click an empty seat to call its person back.
