A group health jam

Office Pulse

Every teammate makes one office noise: ringing phones, coughs, the coffee machine, typing, clicking, the claims stamp, a pen on a glass, the photocopier and the washroom flush. Thirteen people, each with their own seat. Call them in and their noises lock into one groove.

Hover over someone to see who they are. Click them to send them home, or click an empty seat to call its person back.
